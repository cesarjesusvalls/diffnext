      How the two are aligned

      Both geometries sit in the nexus frame of Next100OpticalGeometry, in millimetres: the gate is at z = 0 and z grows towards the cathode. The LUCiD cylinder is placed on the light-tube axis, with its readout cap on the SiPM sensitive surface (z = −24.13 mm) and its PMT cap on the sapphire-window face (z = 1458.67 mm).

      In nexus the tracking and energy planes are centred (3.7, 6.4) mm off the light-tube axis. LUCiD places them the same way, so every SiPM and every PMT window sits where nexus has it.

      SiPMs Hamamatsu S13372-1350TE, 1.3 × 1.3 mm sensitive area, 15.55 mm pitch, 3584 on 56 boards; 3130 have their centre inside the light-tube radius. Sensitive surface at z = −24.1 mm. Those 3130, as flat 1.3 × 1.3 mm squares at the nexus positions, with the nexus ids, on the readout cap at z = −24.13 mm.
      Around each SiPM A 6 × 6 mm hole in a 6 mm TPB-coated teflon mask; the sensitive area sits 5.2 mm below the mask face (z = −18.9 mm). No mask: SiPMs and a reflecting wall share one plane.
      Where S2 light is made The EL gap, between the anode mesh (z = −10.1) and the gate mesh (z = −0.1 mm): 14–24 mm from the SiPM surfaces. No electrodes. Point sources go wherever they are placed, for example 14–24 mm from the readout cap.
      Cathode end Hexagonal steel mesh at z = 1187.4 mm, then a 271 mm buffer with its own light tube up to the PMT windows. No cathode: the mesh is fully open, so the drift region and the buffer are one volume, with the side wall continuing to the PMT windows.
      Energy plane 60 PMTs behind 80 mm TPB-coated sapphire windows, faces at z = 1458.7 mm, standing 9 mm out of a copper plate. 60 flat 80 mm discs at the window positions, on the PMT cap at z = 1458.67 mm; the cap reflects between them. Twelve reach past the cylinder radius and can only be hit on their inner part.
      Gas Xenon at 13.5 bar; nexus gives it no absorption length and no Rayleigh table. Same: scattering and absorption lengths of 10⁹ m.
      Surface optics Teflon: Lambertian, reflectivity 0.94 (visible) and 0.72 (VUV), TPB wavelength shifting. Copper between the PMT windows: 0.2–0.4, mostly specular. One Lambertian reflectivity, 0.94, for every wall, no wavelength shifting. SiPMs and PMT windows absorb every photon that reaches them.
