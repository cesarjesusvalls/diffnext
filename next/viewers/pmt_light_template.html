What is being computed

  • Geometry: a cylinder of radius 491.65 mm, 1482.8 mm from the SiPM plane to the PMT windows. The 3130 SiPMs (1.3 mm squares) and 60 PMT windows (80 mm discs) sit at their NEXT-100 positions; the windows on the rim can only be hit on their inner part. The cathode mesh is not modelled.
  • Optics: every wall reflects 94% of the light, diffusely. SiPMs and PMT windows absorb every ray that reaches them. The gas neither scatters nor absorbs.
  • Direct is the first leg of each ray; total follows each ray through up to 200 reflections; indirect is total minus direct. Each source emits 10⁶ rays on a Fibonacci lattice.
  • Source positions are measured from the gate towards the cathode (at 1.19 m), and x from the light-tube axis, with y = 0.
